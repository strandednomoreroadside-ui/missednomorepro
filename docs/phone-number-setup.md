# Getting your phone set up with Missed No More Pro

Your AI receptionist answers calls on a phone number. You have two ways to do
this — **pick the one that fits your business.** Both take about 5 minutes.

> **Quick recommendation**
> - **Just starting, or happy to advertise a new number?** → **Option A (new number).** Simplest.
> - **Have an existing number on your trucks, Google listing, and ads?** → **Option B (keep your number + forwarding).** Most popular.

---

## Option A — Get a brand-new number through us (simplest)

We give you a local phone number that the AI answers directly. Nothing to
configure on your end.

**How it works**
1. We assign you a local number (you'll see it under **Dashboard → Numbers**).
2. Start using it: put it on your website, Google Business Profile, business
   cards, truck wraps, and ads.
3. Every call to that number is answered by your AI receptionist 24/7.

**Best for:** new businesses, a dedicated "after-hours" or "overflow" line, or
anyone who doesn't mind publishing a new number.

**Trade-off:** if you already advertise a different number everywhere, you'd
have to update those listings — in that case, use Option B instead.

---

## Option B — Keep your current number (call forwarding)

Keep the number customers already know. You just tell your phone carrier to
**forward calls to the number we give you**, and the AI answers them. Your
published number never changes.

**Step 1 — Get your forwarding target.** In **Dashboard → Numbers** you'll see
the number we assigned you (it looks like `+1 216 555 0142`). That's the number
you forward *to*.

**Step 2 — Pick how much the AI should handle:**

| Mode | What happens | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Forward all calls** | Every call goes straight to the AI. | "I want nothing missed" — the AI is your front desk. |
| **Forward when busy / no answer** | Your phone rings first; if you don't pick up in ~4 rings (or you're on another call), it rolls to the AI. | Owners who still want to answer personally when they can, with the AI as backup. |

**Step 3 — Turn on forwarding with your carrier.** Exact steps vary by
carrier — these are the common ones. **If a code doesn't work, call your carrier
and ask them to "forward my calls to `<the number from Step 1>`."**

*Cell phones (most carriers — confirm with yours):*
- **Forward all calls:** dial `*72` then the number, press call. (Some carriers
  use `**21*` + number + `#`.) Turn off with `*73` (or `##21#`).
- **Forward only when unanswered:** `*71` or `**61*` + number + `#`. Turn off
  with `##61#`.
- **Forward only when busy:** `**67*` + number + `#`. Turn off with `##67#`.
- Or just open your carrier's app (Verizon / AT&T / T-Mobile) → **Call
  Forwarding** and paste the number — no codes needed.

*Landline / VoIP / office systems (RingCentral, Ooma, Spectrum, etc.):*
- Look for **Call Forwarding** in your account settings or admin portal and
  point it at the number from Step 1. Most support "forward all" and
  "forward on no-answer/busy."

**Step 4 — Test it.** Call your own published number from another phone. The AI
should pick up (immediately for "forward all," or after a few rings for
"no answer"). The call will appear in **Dashboard → Calls** within a minute.

**Best for:** established businesses with a number already on their listings,
trucks, and ads. **This is what most customers choose.**

## Option C — Move (port) your number to us (later)

If you'd rather we *host* your existing number directly (no forwarding), we can
transfer it to our system. Porting takes about **1–2 weeks**, your number keeps
working the whole time, and we handle the paperwork. Email
**support@missednomorepro.com** to start — we do these by hand during early
access.

## Frequently asked

**Will I still get my calls / see who called?** Yes. Every call — answered by
the AI, forwarded, or missed — shows in **Dashboard → Calls** with a summary,
and you get a text alert on new leads.

**Can I answer some calls myself?** Yes — use the "forward when busy / no
answer" mode (Option B), so your phone rings first and the AI only catches the
ones you miss.

**Does forwarding cost me extra?** Forwarding itself is usually free on modern
plans, but check your carrier. Your Missed No More Pro plan includes the AI
number and a monthly pool of talk minutes (see your plan).

**What if I hit my plan's minutes?** Calls forward straight to your phone so you
never miss one, and we prompt you to upgrade — there's never a surprise overage
charge.
