# 30-Day Execution Calendar

The day-by-day checklist. It ties every channel together into one routine.
Strategy and targets live in `05`. This is what you actually do each day.

Rule from the math: a 7-day trial started after about day 23 will not convert inside the sprint.
So acquisition front-loads into Weeks 1 to 3. Week 4 is a conversion sprint, not an acquisition week.

---

## WEEK 0 — Build once, then the machine runs

Do this in one or two focused sessions. Nothing ships until the exit gate passes.

- [ ] Stand up the public demo number and call it yourself to confirm it works. See README step 1.
- [ ] Fill the 4 variables into your saved copy of each asset. See README step 2.
- [ ] Optimize the LinkedIn profile using the pack in `02`: headline, banner, About, Featured.
- [ ] Set up the Facebook page, pinned post from `04`, and request to join 20 to 30 owner groups from `04`.
- [ ] Create the free tool stack accounts: Metricool, HubSpot Free, MailerLite, PostHog, Make.com. See `05`.
- [ ] Batch-write and schedule 20 to 30 posts in Metricool, pulling from `02` and `04`.
- [ ] Load the pre-trial email sequence from `06` into MailerLite.
- [ ] Build the outreach board in Airtable or Notion, load 300 to 500 ICP targets, load templates into TextBlaze. See `03`.
- [ ] Turn on the product's own trial onboarding, Test-my-AI, and review automations. Dogfood it.
- [ ] Wire the product webhooks to Make to your CRM and the weekly dashboard sheet from `05`.
- [ ] Set the founder-slots counter and build the one-screen weekly dashboard from `05` section 3.

Exit gate: stack live, demo call works, 20 posts queued, 300 targets loaded, one test lead flows all
the way from source to CRM to dashboard.

## The default daily routine, Weeks 1 to 3

About 30 to 45 minutes. This is the repeatable block. Specific days below add to it.

- [ ] Your scheduled post publishes automatically. Reply to every comment within a few hours.
- [ ] Send 15 to 20 LinkedIn touches from `03`: connection notes and DMs to operators and partners.
- [ ] Leave 5 to 10 value comments in Facebook owner groups, especially on "I keep missing calls" threads. See `04`.
- [ ] Send a few warm Facebook DMs only to people who raised a hand. Never cold-blast.
- [ ] Move every reply forward one stage on the outreach board. Route warm ones to the demo number.
- [ ] Glance at the daily metric: demo-AI calls. If it is on pace, keep going. If flat, fix that first.

## WEEK 1 — Launch and seed demo calls
KPI this week: demo-AI calls.

- [ ] Day 1: Announce the Founder's Offer, 10 slots. Publish the launch post from `02` and `04`. Run the daily routine.
- [ ] Day 1: Turn on the Meta ad test, about $15/day, straight to the demo landing page. Tight home-service targeting. See `04`.
- [ ] Days 2 to 5: Daily routine. Post the missed-call-math piece and the "call it and judge it" post this week.
- [ ] Days 2 to 5: Watch which message and which ad drive demo calls. Note the winner.
- [ ] Days 6 to 7: Lighter posting. Keep outreach going. Reply to everything.

Week 1 checkpoint, cumulative: reach ~12k, demo calls ~10, leads ~35, trials ~5 to 7, paying ~1 to 3.

---

## WEEK 2 — Optimize and find what converts
KPI this week: trial starts and activation.

- [ ] Kill the losing ad creative. Put the ~$15/day on the winner. See `04` kill and scale rules.
- [ ] Double down on the outreach angle that produced demo calls.
- [ ] First trial cohort hits its 7-day decision. The product's own SMS and email push activation and Test-my-AI.
- [ ] Publish your first auto-collected 5-star review to the landing page. Loop 2 starts paying off.
- [ ] Bottleneck check: is leads or trial-starts pinching harder? Reallocate. See `05` section 7.

Week 2 checkpoint, cumulative: reach ~30k, demo calls ~26, leads ~90, trials ~14, activated ~9, paying ~5 to 7.

---

## WEEK 3 — Scale the winner and trigger referrals
KPI this week: outbound volume and leads. This is the last heavy trial-START week.

- [ ] Push outreach to 120 to 150 touches this week on the proven message.
- [ ] Spend the remaining ad budget, about $100 to $150, on the single best creative to the demo page.
- [ ] Seed demo-clip shares into Facebook owner groups. Loop 1.
- [ ] Fire the first referral asks to activated customers. Loop 3. See `05` section 5.
- [ ] Amplify scarcity as founder slots visibly fill.

Week 3 checkpoint, cumulative: reach ~50 to 60k, demo calls ~45, leads ~150, trials ~24, paying ~10 to 13.

---

## WEEK 4 — Close and sprint the Founder Offer
KPI this week: trial-to-paid and paying customers. Conversion, not acquisition.

- [ ] Full-court press on active trials near their decision. Activation nudges plus Test-my-AI plus last-slots scarcity.
- [ ] Send a referral ask to every activated customer.
- [ ] Point final ad dollars at retargeting site visitors and demo-callers who did not trial.
- [ ] Run the trial-to-paid nurture from `06` on every trial in its final days.
- [ ] Seed 3 to 5 B2B partner conversations for after the sprint.

Day 30 review: base 11 to 13 paying, stretch 20 to 24. Keep what worked running. Trials started on
days 24 to 30 convert in early Week 5, so momentum carries past the sprint.

---

## If something stalls

The fix table is in `05` section 7. The golden rule: fix whatever is highest in the funnel that is
off-target first. A leak at demo calls makes every downstream fix worthless.
