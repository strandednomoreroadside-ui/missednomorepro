# 06 — Objection Battlecard + Automated Nurture

**Owner:** Sales/Conversion lane. **Depends on:** `00-campaign-foundation.md` (that doc wins on any conflict).
**Purpose:** Make the written copy close, with near-zero founder time. Everything here assumes the
**self-serve, card-on-file 7-day trial IS the close** and the anchor CTAs are **"hear the AI — call it now"**
and **"start your free trial."**

Guardrails baked into every line below (from foundation §9):
- Never claim the AI is human. Say "AI receptionist" / "Grace, our AI."
- Never claim it invents or negotiates prices. It quotes **the owner's own price sheet**, computed
  server-side — it physically can't make up a number.
- Never imply the trial needs no card. It's **7 days, card required, ~30-min voice cap during trial.**
- Pricing is a **HARD CAP** — no metered overage, no surprise bills. At the cap, calls forward to the owner.
- Founder's Offer: **first 10 businesses to go paid get every paid add-on free for life while subscribed.**
  Use the live remaining-slots count as urgency.

## PART 1 — Objection-Handling Battlecard (12)

Format: the objection as the owner actually says it → the plain-English answer → **where this response lives.**
Keep answers short enough to drop straight into a DM or an ad.

### 1. "AI will sound robotic / fake."
**Answer:** Don't take my word for it — **call it right now and talk to it.** (440) 644-2423. It's a real,
human-sounding voice (we named her Grace). If it sounded like a robot, we'd never tell you to call it. Have a
full conversation, try to trip it up, then decide. Ten seconds on the phone beats any promise I could make.
**Lives in:** Ad copy (primary hook), DM (first reply to any skeptic), Landing FAQ (top spot), Day-1 trial email.
_This is the whole strategy — for a receptionist product, "just call it" is the close. Always route this
objection to the phone, never to more words._

### 2. "My customers want a real human, not a machine."
**Answer:** Totally fair — and right now, when you're under a sink or on a roof, the "human" your customer
actually gets is **voicemail** (or a busy signal). Grace isn't competing with you picking up — she's
competing with the call you **can't** take. She answers warmly, books the job or gets a price, and the
second something needs a real person she **texts you the lead instantly** or transfers the call. Your
customer gets helped in seconds instead of dialing your competitor.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, DM (this is the #1 emotional objection — handle it with the reframe, not stats),
Nurture email #2 (pre-trial).
_Strongest reframe in the deck — see summary._

### 3. "Too expensive / I'm just a one-man shop."
**Answer:** One booked job usually covers the whole month. Starter is **$99/mo** — that's less than the
value of a **single** job you'd lose to voicemail. Miss two calls a week and it's not a cost, it's the
cheapest employee you'll ever hire (one who works 24/7 and never calls in sick). And it's a **hard cap** —
you'll never get a surprise bill. Run the free trial for 7 days and let it pay for itself before you decide.
**Lives in:** Ad copy, Landing FAQ, DM, Day-5 trial email (value recap before the card charges).

### 4. "I already have voicemail."
**Answer:** Here's the problem: **about 8 out of 10 people won't leave a voicemail** — they just hang up and
call the next company on Google. Voicemail is where leads go to die. Grace *answers*, books the job, and if
she can't, she **texts the caller back in seconds** so they never bounce. Voicemail catches messages;
Grace catches jobs.
**Lives in:** Ad copy, Landing FAQ, Nurture email #1 (pre-trial), DM.

### 5. "I already use an answering service."
**Answer:** How much are you paying, and does it actually **book the job** — or just take a message and email
it to you? Most services are a call center reading a script; they can't quote your prices or drop a job on
your calendar. Grace answers 24/7 with **your** price sheet, **books straight into your calendar**,
dispatches, and texts back missed calls — usually for less than a per-call answering service. Call the demo
line and compare it to what you've got.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, DM, LinkedIn (multi-location / higher-spend operators), Nurture email #3.

### 6. "I don't really miss that many calls."
**Answer:** Easy test: of the calls you missed last week, how many left a voicemail? Almost none, right? So
you don't actually know how many you missed — you only see the ones who bothered to leave a message. The
trial makes it visible: every call gets answered and logged, so after 7 days you'll see the real number in
black and white. Most owners are surprised (and a little annoyed at what they'd been losing).
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, DM, Nurture email #4 (pre-trial), Day-3 trial email ("look at your call log").

### 7. "I don't have time to set it up."
**Answer:** Setup is minutes, not a project. Sign up, connect your number and your calendar, and hit
**"Test my AI"** — the system calls *you* so you hear your own receptionist answering for your own business.
No install, no new hardware, no IT. If you can forward a call, you can go live. And you keep your existing
number.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, Day-1 trial email (activation), DM, Trial reminder SMS.

### 8. "Will it mess up my phone or my Google listing?"
**Answer:** No. You keep your number and your listing exactly as they are. You just forward calls to Grace
(all the time, or only when you can't pick up — your choice). Nothing changes on Google, nothing changes on
your phone, and you can switch it off anytime. It sits *behind* your number, it doesn't replace it.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, DM, Day-2 trial email (the "connect your number" step), phone-setup guide.

### 9. "What if it quotes the wrong price?"
**Answer:** It can't invent a price — that's the whole point. Grace only reads back numbers computed from
**your** price sheet (you approve every rule: your dispatch zones, your rates, your add-ons). If a job's too
weird to price, she says she'll have you follow up instead of guessing. No made-up numbers, ever — it's
built so the AI literally cannot pull a price out of thin air.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, DM (kills the #1 trust fear for trades), Day-4 trial email (reassurance).

### 10. "I'm not a tech person."
**Answer:** Good news — you don't have to be. It's plain-English screens, one step at a time, and if you can
type your prices into a form (or just upload your existing price sheet and let it read them for you), you're
done. No coding, no wiring, no manual. And "Test my AI" lets you *hear* it working before you trust it with
a real customer. Built for people who work with their hands, not a keyboard.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, DM, Nurture email #3, Day-1 trial email.

### 11. "What happens after 7 days — am I gonna get surprise-billed?"
**Answer:** Straight answer: yes, the card is required, and if you do nothing the trial rolls into your
chosen plan at the end of day 7 — we tell you the date up front and remind you before it happens. Cancel
anytime in one click before then and you're not charged a cent. After that it's flat monthly pricing with a
**hard cap** — no per-minute meter, no bill shock. If you ever hit your plan's cap, calls just forward to
you. What you see is what you pay.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ (must be crystal-clear — FTC + trust), Day-5 and Day-6 trial emails, billing page.
_Never soften this. Transparency here converts more than it scares off; hidden terms cause chargebacks._

### 12. "Does it *actually* book jobs into my calendar, or just take messages?"
**Answer:** It actually books. Connect your Google Calendar and Grace checks your real availability, offers
open slots, books the appointment, dispatches the tech, and texts the customer a confirmation — all during
the call. It's not a voicemail-with-extra-steps; it's a receptionist that closes the loop. Run "Test my AI,"
pretend to be a customer, and watch the appointment land on your calendar.
**Lives in:** Landing FAQ, Ad copy (differentiator), Day-2 trial email (connect calendar), DM.

## PART 2 — Trial → Paid Nurture (7-day, fully automated)

**Objective:** get every trial to (a) **hear the AI** ("Test my AI"), (b) **connect number + calendar**,
and (c) reach the end of day 7 **activated and glad to convert** — before the card charges. Reinforce the
Founder's Offer scarcity throughout.

**Rules:** Email + SMS. Keep it short and trades-friendly. Every message has ONE action. SMS includes
STOP/HELP compliance (these go to trial users who gave their number at signup — transactional onboarding).
The card auto-charges at the end of day 7, so "convert" here = **activate + don't cancel.**

**Branching (automation, not extra founder work):** the sequence below is the default. Suppress the
"activation" nudges for anyone who's already done the step (see Part 5 triggers). If someone has **not** run
"Test my AI" by Day 3, that's the top-priority nudge — everything else waits behind it.

## PART 4 — Hot-Lead Close Checklist (founder runs it in ~2 minutes)

For the rare prospect who DMs back or asks for a human. **Do NOT turn this into a sales call.** The goal is
one thing: get them to **hear the AI** and **start the trial**, today. Copy-paste, personalize the bracket,
send.

1. **Acknowledge + qualify in one line:** "Appreciate you reaching out — quick one so I point you right: are
   you the owner, and roughly how many calls a day do you figure you miss?"
2. **Route to the demo (do this before anything else):** "Best 60 seconds you'll spend today — call this
   number and talk to the AI receptionist yourself: (440) 644-2423. Try to trip her up, then tell me what you
   think."
3. **Answer at most ONE objection** with the matching battlecard line (usually #2 human, #3 price, or #9
   pricing). Keep it to 2-3 sentences. Don't over-explain.
4. **Move to the trial immediately:** "Easiest way to see it on *your* calls is the 7-day free trial — card
   required, ~30-min voice cap, hard cap after so no surprise bills, cancel in one click. Here's the link:
   [start trial]."
5. **Drop the scarcity:** "Heads up — first 10 businesses to go paid get every paid add-on free for life.
   10 slots left, so starting now locks it in."
6. **Offer to watch the first setup step, not run it:** "Sign up and I'll make sure your 'Test my AI' call
   comes through clean — reply here once you're in."
7. **Set a single follow-up if no action in 24h:** "Still want me to hold a Founder's slot? Just say the
   word and hit https://missednomorepro.com/signup — otherwise no worries, I'll leave you to it."

_If they push for a full demo call: "Honestly, the demo *is* the AI — call [number] and you've had it, no
scheduling needed. If you're still stuck after the trial, then I'll jump on with you."_

## PART 5 — Activation Triggers to Watch (predict conversion → nudge the right behavior)

These are the in-product signals that a trial will convert. Nurture and any founder attention should chase
**#1 above everything.** Wire these to suppress/trigger the Part-2 messages.

1. **Ran "Test my AI" (heard Grace answer for their own business).** — The single biggest predictor. If they
   haven't done this by Day 3, it's the ONLY thing the next message should ask for. Hearing it is believing
   it; nothing else converts until this happens.
2. **Connected the calendar (and/or forwarded their number).** — Signals real intent to *use* it, not just
   kick tires. Someone who wired up call forwarding is planning to run live calls through it. If missing by
   Day 2–3, that's the next nudge after #1.
3. **First real inbound call answered by the AI, or a missed-call text-back fired.** — The "aha": the system
   caught a live lead. Once this happens, shift messaging from "try it" to "look what it just did for you"
   and "lock your plan." A booked appointment during the trial is near-certain conversion.
4. **Approved their price sheet / added FAQs (or uploaded a price doc).** — Setup investment = intent to
   keep it. Owners who've entered their real prices and answers have mentally moved in; point them straight
   at "pick your plan + claim a Founder's slot."

**Rule of thumb for the automation:** No #1 → rescue with the "hear it" nudge, hold everything else. #1 done
but no #2 → push the connect step. #1 + #2 + #3 → switch entirely to value-recap + convert + scarcity. All
four → they're a lock; just confirm the plan and the Founder's slot.
