## PART 1 — FACEBOOK GROUPS STRATEGY (the main organic engine)

Facebook Groups are where our exact buyer actually hangs out and asks for help. This is the highest-leverage,
lowest-cost channel in the whole campaign. It is also the easiest place to get **banned or removed** if we act
like a salesperson. The entire game is: **be the most genuinely helpful person in the group, and let the
product surface naturally when it's the honest answer to a real question.**

### 1.1 The five group categories (join in this priority order)

**Tier A — Trade-specific OWNER / business groups (highest intent, join first).**
These people own the exact businesses we serve and talk about operations, phones, and lost jobs daily.
Search these patterns in the FB search bar → filter to **Groups** → sort by members/activity:
- `HVAC business owners`, `HVAC contractors`, `Plumbing business owners`, `Electricians business network`,
  `Roofing business owners / roofers`, `Garage door business`, `Appliance repair business owners`,
  `Lawn care / landscaping business owners`, `Pressure washing business`, `Cleaning business owners`
  (residential + commercial), `Pest control business owners`, `Pool service business`, `Handyman business`,
  `Painting business owners`, `Junk removal business`, `Roadside / towing business owners`.
- Also search `[trade] entrepreneurs`, `[trade] mastermind`, `[trade] tips and tricks`, `women in the trades`.

**Tier B — General home-service / contractor GROWTH groups (coach-run, high buyer intent).**
Owners in these groups are actively spending money to grow — perfect fit.
- Coach/brand communities like **"Home Service Millionaire"**-style groups, Contractor Growth / Service
  Business Growth communities, "trades business owners" masterminds, "service business mastery," "scaling
  the trades," "blue-collar business," "home service freedom / scaling" groups.
- Note: many are gated behind the coach's paid program or have strict no-promo rules. Play these **pure
  value, zero pitch** — the payoff is relationships and DMs, not link drops.

**Tier C — Software-adjacent groups (they already buy tools).**
People here have already accepted that software runs their business — a warm audience for "the thing that
answers your phone."
- `Jobber users / community`, `Housecall Pro users`, `ServiceTitan users`, `QuickBooks for contractors`,
  `Google Local Services Ads for contractors`, CRM/field-service software groups.

**Tier D — Local / regional + networking groups (relationship sell, slow but sticky).**
- Local **Chamber of Commerce**, **BNI** chapter groups, `[City] small business owners`,
  `[City/County] contractors / trades`, `[State] home services`. Lower volume, higher trust, easy referrals.

**Tier E — Broad small-business groups (volume, lower fit — join a couple, don't over-invest).**
- `Small business owners`, `Local business marketing`, `Small business support` communities.

**Realistic target:** join **20–30 groups** total across A–E over the first week (5–8/day so you don't trip
FB's spam heuristics). Prioritize groups with (a) 3k–80k members, (b) multiple posts per day, and
(c) rules that permit value posts. Skip dead groups and pure link-spam graveyards.

### 1.2 How to get accepted (and not flagged on day one)

1. **Fix the profile first — the profile IS the landing page.** Admins vet you before approving.
   - Real name + real face photo. Cover photo can subtly say what you do.
   - Bio / intro / "work" field: *"Helping home-service owners stop losing jobs to voicemail — AI that answers
     every call 24/7."* Put `https://missednomorepro.com/signup` in the profile links, **not** in group posts.
   - Have a few real posts already up so the profile doesn't look like a burner.
2. **Answer the membership questions honestly.** Many groups ask "are you here to sell?" — answer
   *"No — I run a business in the home-service space and I'm here to learn and help."* That's true. Do **not**
   pitch in the answers; that's an instant reject.
3. **Warm up before posting.** For the first 3–5 days in a new group: react, comment helpfully, and ask a
   genuine question or two. Post value **after** you've shown you're a member, not a drive-by.
4. **Read the rules and pin them in a note.** Track per group: promo allowed? links allowed? "promo day"?
   self-promo threads? Some groups have a weekly promo thread — that's your sanctioned pitch window.
5. **Build relationships with admins.** A 30-second "thanks for running this group, it's genuinely useful"
   DM to an admin is worth more than 10 posts. Several will let you post value (or even do a paid shoutout —
   often $20–100, which fits the budget) once they trust you.

### 1.3 The value-first posting rules (90% value / 10% pitch)

- **90/10, and even the 10 is soft.** Nine of every ten touches give something away free: an answer, a number,
  a template, a hard-won lesson. Only the tenth even hints at the product — and only when it's the honest
  answer to what someone asked.
- **Comments > posts.** The safest, highest-converting move is being the best answer on *someone else's*
  post ("how do you handle calls while you're on a job?" is asked constantly). You ride their reach, you look
  helpful not promotional, and you almost never get removed for a helpful comment.
- **Never drop a cold link in a post.** Links tank reach and trip spam filters. Use the **"comment and I'll
  DM you"** mechanic instead: *"Happy to share the missed-call math I used — comment 'CALLS' and I'll send it."*
  This is opt-in, so the DM is welcome, not spam.
- **The product surfaces as a P.S., not a pitch.** e.g. after a genuinely useful answer: *"(Full disclosure:
  I'm biased — I work on an AI phone answerer. But even if you never use one, the fixes above are free.)"*
  Transparency disarms the "he's just selling" reflex and admins respect it.
- **Let people ask you.** When you post the missed-call math or a real result, owners will comment "what do
  you use?" That question is your permission slip — answer it in the thread or DM, not before.
- **One value post per group per week, max.** Spread posts across your 20–30 groups so no single group sees
  you as a repeat promoter. Rotate the topic.
- **Screenshots and numbers travel.** A screen recording of the AI booking a job, or a plain math breakdown,
  gets shared and saved. A wall of text asking people to "check out my product" gets deleted.

### 1.4 How to convert without getting banned

- **The DM is where the sale warms up — but only after opt-in.** Someone comments "CALLS" or "AI" → you DM
  them the promised value first (the math, a link to hear the demo), *then* one soft line: *"If you want to
  actually hear it, call `(440) 644-2423` — it's our AI live. And there's a free 7-day trial if you like it."*
- **Push "hear it," not "buy it."** For a receptionist, "call this number and listen" outperforms any pitch
  and needs zero founder time. The demo call and the "Test my AI" trial button do the selling.
- **Respect promo days / rules religiously.** One ban kills a whole group's worth of reach. When in doubt,
  ask the admin "is this okay to share here?" — asking permission builds trust even when the answer is no.
- **Track opt-ins in a simple sheet** (name, group, what they asked, did they call the demo, did they trial).
  This is your warm list and it's free.

4. **Proof / build-in-public** (founder honesty, small wins, screen recordings — trust).

## PART 3 — THE $0–500 META AD TEST (one tight campaign)

**Philosophy for a $500 budget:** this is **validation, not a growth channel.** $500 will not "scale."
Its job is to answer one question cheaply: *does a cold home-service owner, shown the missed-call pain, click
through and either call the demo or start a trial?* We optimize ruthlessly, kill fast, and treat any trial as
gravy. Organic (Parts 1–2) is the real engine.

### 3.1 Objective — **Traffic, optimized for Landing Page Views** (with a clear reason)

- **Recommended: Traffic objective, optimized for "Landing page views" (not link clicks).** Reasons:
  1. **The product demos itself.** Our whole edge is "hear it / try it." We want warm bodies actually *on the
     page* where the demo number + "start trial" buttons live — then the self-serve funnel closes them with
     zero founder time. Traffic → landing is the cleanest path to the self-demo.
  2. **Leads (instant form) creates founder work.** A lead form just harvests names/emails — then *someone
     has to follow up*, which violates the "minimal founder time / no book-a-call" constraint. We'd rather
     send clicks straight to the self-serve trial than build a lead list we have to work by hand.
  3. **$500 is too little for the Sales/Conversions objective to optimize.** Conversion optimization needs
     ~50 events/week to learn; we won't hit that. Traffic gets us data and warm pixel audiences now.
  - "Landing page views" (vs. link clicks) makes Meta optimize for people who actually **load the page**, not
    accidental tappers — better signal for our money.
- **Install the Meta Pixel first** and fire two custom events on the landing page: `DemoCallClick`
  (tap-to-call the demo number) and `TrialStart` (reaches the trial signup). Even if we don't optimize toward
  them, we need them to **measure** the test and to **build retargeting audiences** for later.
- **Upgrade path (post-test, if it works):** once the pixel has logged ~30–50 `TrialStart` or `DemoCallClick`
  events, a future, better-funded campaign can switch to the **Sales/Leads objective optimized for that event.**
  Not now.

### 3.2 Audience targeting (nationwide US home-service owners)

Run **two ad sets** so we learn which targeting wins on our budget:

**Ad Set A — Detailed targeting ("owners of these businesses"):**
- Location: **United States** (nationwide).
- Age 28–60, all genders.
- **Detailed targeting (layer "business owner" + "trade/field-service" signals; use `Narrow audience`/"must
  also match"):**
  - Layer 1 (who they are): *Small business owners*, *Business owners*, *Business management*,
    *Entrepreneurship*.
  - Layer 2 — **narrow to** (what they do / tools they use): *Jobber*, *Housecall Pro*, *ServiceTitan*,
    *Contractor*, *General contractor*, *Plumbing*, *HVAC*, *Electrician*, *Roofer*, *Landscaping*,
    *Home improvement*, *Field service management*. (Include whichever interests Meta actually surfaces —
    availability varies.)
- Exclude: current page engagers you've already got (avoid overlap) — optional at this budget.

**Ad Set B — Broad + creative-led (let the algorithm find them):**
- Location: **United States**. Age 28–60. **No detailed targeting** (or only "Small business owners").

- **Placements: Advantage+ Placements (automatic).** Let Meta spread across Feed/Reels/Stories; don't
  hand-pick on this budget. Make sure creative works muted + vertical-friendly.

### 3.4 Budget, structure, and schedule

- **Total cap: $500.** Never exceed. Set an **account spending limit of $500** in Ads Manager so it physically
  can't overspend.
- **Structure:** 1 campaign → 2 ad sets (A detailed, B broad) → 3 ads each (same 3 creatives). Use
  **CBO / Advantage campaign budget** so Meta shifts spend to whatever's working.
- **Phase 1 — Test (Days 1–7): $15/day** (≈$105). Learn which creative + audience combo gets the cheapest,
  highest-quality landing-page views and any demo/trial events.
- **Phase 2 — Concentrate (Days 8–18): $18–20/day** (≈$210) on the **winning ad + winning audience only**
  (pause the rest). This is where a winner gets a fair shot.
- **Reserve: ~$185 held back** for (a) retargeting the pixel audience the test built (people who hit the
  landing page but didn't trial — show them AD 3 scarcity), or (b) extending the winner a few more days if
  it's clearly converting. **Only spend the reserve if Phase 2 shows a real signal.**
- If nothing works by ~$150 spent, **stop and reallocate the remaining budget to a small admin/promo-day
  budget for group posts** (paying a couple of group admins $20–100 for a value shoutout is often a better
  use of the money than more cold Meta reach).

### 3.5 Kill / scale rules (decide before emotions get involved)

**Kill an ad (pause it) when, after ~$25–30 spend OR ~1,500 impressions, ANY of:**
- CTR (link) **< 0.8%** (the hook isn't landing), or
- Cost per landing-page view **> ~$1.50–2.00** with **zero** `DemoCallClick`/`TrialStart` events, or
- It's clearly the worst of the three on both CTR and cost-per-LPV.

**Scale the winner (raise budget ~20–30% every 2–3 days) when:**
- CTR **> 1.5%**, and
- Cost per landing-page view is at/below your target, and
- It's producing `DemoCallClick` and/or `TrialStart` events (even a few).
- Never more than ~30%/day increase (bigger jumps reset Meta's learning and spike CPMs).

**Refresh creative when:** frequency **> 2.5** (same people seeing it too often) and performance decays —
swap the image/hook, keep the winning angle.

### 3.6 What "winning" looks like (be honest about attribution at this budget)

At $500, clean last-click attribution to a paid trial is unreliable — a lot of value shows up as demo calls,
direct trials, and warmed-up group prospects. So judge on a **ladder of leading indicators**, best to worst:

1. **Best:** ≥ 1 paid customer clearly attributable to ads within the $500 (CAC ≤ ~$250 — under one Starter
   year — is a green light to fund it further later). Even **1–3 trials** at this budget = the angle works.
2. **Good:** meaningful **`DemoCallClick` / `TrialStart` events** (people are self-demoing — the funnel works,
   just needs volume). A cost per trial-start under ~$40–60 is promising for a future scaled test.
3. **Signal:** CTR **> 1.5%** + cost-per-landing-page-view under ~$1 on the winning creative — the *message*
   resonates with cold owners even if conversions are thin; that de-risks all the organic copy too.
4. **Kill:** none of the above by ~$150 spent → the paid channel isn't ready; pour energy into groups/DMs and
   pocket the rest of the budget.

**The real prize even in the "kill" case:** the test tells us **which hook and which audience convert**, and
it builds a **retargeting pixel audience** we can hit for pennies later. That intel makes every organic post
and every future ad better. $150 to learn that is cheap.
