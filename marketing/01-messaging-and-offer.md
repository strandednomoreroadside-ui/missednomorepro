## 1. The Grand-Slam Offer

### 1a. The value equation (why this is a no-brainer for a home-service owner)

```
              Dream Outcome  ×  Perceived Likelihood of Getting It
  Value  =  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    Time Delay  ×  Effort & Sacrifice
```

**Dream outcome (numerator — pull it UP)**
Never lose another job to a missed call. Every call answered, every lead captured, jobs booked
straight into the calendar — even while you're under a sink, on a roof, or asleep. You sound like
a bigger, more professional shop, and you get your phone (and your evenings) back. The buyer isn't
paying for "software." They're buying **the jobs they've been handing their competitor for free.**

**Perceived likelihood of achievement (numerator — pull it UP)**
This is the whole reason the offer wins: **the product proves itself before they pay a dime.**
- They can **call it right now** and have a real conversation with the AI. No pitch, no "trust me."
- Pricing has a **hard cap** — no per-minute meter, no bill shock. At the cap, calls just forward
  to the owner.
- It **physically cannot invent a price** — every quote is computed from the owner's own price sheet.
- **7-day free trial** to watch it answer their own calls before they're charged.
Belief goes from "AI will embarrass me in front of my customers" to "I heard it — it's good."

**Time delay (denominator — push it DOWN)**
Sign up → connect your number and calendar → go live. **Same day.** First missed-call text-back and
first booked call can happen within hours, not weeks. There's no install, no hardware, no waiting on
a rep to "get you scheduled."

**Effort & sacrifice (denominator — push it DOWN)**
Self-serve. **No mandatory sales call.** The AI does the answering, booking, quoting, texting, and
follow-up. The hard cap means there's nothing to babysit and no surprise invoice to reconcile. The
only real work is a few minutes of setup — and even the price sheet and FAQs can be uploaded from a
document instead of typed.

> **Net:** big dream outcome, high believability (you can literally hear it), near-zero time delay,
> near-zero effort. That's a grand-slam offer — an owner feels dumb saying no once they've heard it.

## 2. Lead Magnets (built for a self-serve funnel)

### PRIMARY — "Call the AI and hear it" (the live demo line)

**Hook:**
> **Call this number and have a real conversation with an AI receptionist — right now. It'll answer
> like it works for a home-service shop. 60 seconds. Then decide.**

**Why it fits self-serve (and why it's the primary):**
For a *receptionist* product, hearing it beats every explanation ever written. It requires **zero
founder time** — the prospect calls a number and the product sells itself. It's the highest-belief,
lowest-effort magnet possible: no form, no download, no wait. It also self-qualifies — anyone who
bothers to call is a real buyer feeling real pain. This magnet directly attacks the biggest objection
("AI will sound like a robot to my customers") by removing all doubt in one phone call.

**Exact next step it drives to:**
End of the call and every asset around it → **"That's your new receptionist. Start your free 7-day
trial and it'll be answering YOUR number today → https://missednomorepro.com/signup."** Once they're in trial, the in-app
**"Test my AI"** button calls *them* so they hear it answering for their own business. Hear it →
trial → hear it on their own number. That's the whole funnel.

## 5. The CTA Ladder (soft → hard), mapped per channel

The two anchors — **hear the AI** and **start free trial** — appear on every channel. Softer rungs
warm the cold ones up to those anchors.
