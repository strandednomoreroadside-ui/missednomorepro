## 1. The goal

**20 paying customers in 30 days**, acquired through **LinkedIn + Facebook only**, on a **lean budget
($0–500 total ad spend)**, with **maximum automation and minimal founder time**.

Honest framing (see `08-metrics-and-funnel-math.md` for the real math): 20-in-30 from a cold start,
organic-first, is a **stretch** goal. The system below is built to give it the best possible shot and to
keep paying off well past day 30. We track a **base case** and a **stretch-to-20 case** so we always know
where we stand.

## 2. What we sell

**Missed No More Pro** (missednomorepro.com) — an **AI voice receptionist + smart CRM + AI business
assistant** built for local home-service businesses (1–15 employees).

A human-sounding AI voice ("Grace") **answers every call 24/7**, and:
- **Books jobs straight into the calendar** and dispatches the tech
- **Quotes exact prices** from the owner's own price sheet (computed server-side — it physically cannot
  make up a number)
- **Instantly texts back missed calls** so the lead doesn't bounce to a competitor
- Runs a full **CRM, follow-ups, review requests, and reputation** engine
- Never sick, never on hold, never sleeps

It's fully self-serve SaaS. Sign up, connect your number and calendar, go live.

## 3. Who we sell to (ICP)

**Owner-operators and small crews (1–15 employees) in the trades / local home services, nationwide US:**
plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, garage door, appliance repair, cleaning, landscaping/lawn, pest
control, pool, handyman, painting, junk removal, roadside/towing, and similar.

**Buyer:** the owner. They answer their own phone, they're on job sites all day, and they *feel* every

They live more on **Facebook** (owner groups) than LinkedIn. LinkedIn is for reaching **multi-location
operators, franchisees, and B2B referral partners** (suppliers, associations, franchise networks).

## 4. The core pain (our one big idea)

> **Every missed call is a job you already paid to earn — handed to your competitor for free.**

Home-service owners miss an estimated **25–40% of inbound calls** (under a sink, on a roof, driving,
asleep). ~**80% of callers won't leave a voicemail** — they just dial the next company. At an average
job value of a few hundred to a couple thousand dollars, a handful of missed calls a week is thousands
in lost revenue every month. Voicemail and "we'll call you back" don't cut it.

Missed No More Pro answers **100% of calls, 24/7**, and turns them into booked jobs.

## 6. The unfair advantage: the product demos itself (automatically)

This is the center of gravity for the whole campaign, because it needs **zero founder time**:

- A prospect can **call our demo number and have a real conversation with the AI right now.**
- Once they start a trial, they hit **"Test my AI"** and the system **calls them** so they hear their
  own receptionist answering for their own business.

For a receptionist product, "just call it and hear it" beats any sales pitch. **Every asset should push
"hear it for yourself."** Seeing (hearing) is believing, and it happens without us on the phone.

## 9. Hard constraints for every asset in this folder

- **Channels:** LinkedIn + Facebook only.
- **Geography:** Nationwide US.
- **ICP:** Broad local home services, owner-operators & small crews (1–15 employees).
- **Budget:** $0–500 total ad spend. Organic + outbound is the engine; paid is a tiny validation test only.
- **Founder time:** MINIMAL. The funnel must carry the prospect all the way to a **self-serve, card-on-file
  7-day trial** with as little human touch as possible. Do **not** design flows whose only CTA is "book a
  call with our team." The primary CTA ladder is: **hear the AI (call it) → start free trial**.
- **Voice/tone:** plain-spoken, trades-friendly, zero corporate jargon, ROI obvious in the first line.
- **Never** claim the AI is human, never promise it invents/negotiates prices, never imply no card is needed
  for the trial.
